Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) is India’s framework for ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) disclosure, introduced by SEBI to align Indian companies with global standards. BRSR replaced the previous Business Responsibility Reports (BRR) in 2021, requiring the top 1,000 listed companies to disclose their non-financial data starting from the fiscal year 2022-2023.

This reporting framework aims to enhance transparency and accountability, driving businesses to adopt sustainable and responsible practices in line with international ESG norms.

**The BRSR (** Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting) **Framework**:

The Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) framework is a pivotal tool designed to enhance the transparency and accountability of businesses in India, particularly in the realm of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) practices. It provides a structured approach for companies to disclose their non-financial data and showcase their commitment to responsible business conduct. The BRSR framework is divided into three key sections, each addressing a different aspect of corporate responsibility.

*Courtesy: ECODRISIL*

Additionally, the BRSR framework is designed to evolve with emerging trends in ESG reporting by dividing disclosures into two categories:

- **Essential Indicators** (*mandatory* ) These are the required disclosures that companies must report on, ensuring compliance with the core ESG criteria.
- **Leadership Indicators** (*voluntary* ) These additional, voluntary disclosures allow businesses to demonstrate leadership in ESG practices, offering a deeper level of transparency and accountability & also ensuring Mutual Trust & confidence & commitment
- Together, these sections of the BRSR framework enable businesses to provide a holistic view of their ESG efforts, paving the way for more sustainable and responsible corporate practices in India.

These metrics provide a holistic view of a company's sustainability initiatives, encompassing its innovations and adoption of responsible practices, its engagement with stakeholders such as customers, suppliers, and communities, and its commitment to fostering inclusive growth and equitable development.

The BRSR framework is guided by nine core principles outlined by the NGRBC; each centred around distinct thematic areas.
