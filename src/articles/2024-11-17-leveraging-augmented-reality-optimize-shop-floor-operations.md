**CRITICAL CONSIDERATIONS WHEN CHOOSING AN AR SOLUTION :Use case** - Selecting the right use case to implement such a solution is critical to ensuring maximum ROI and the best integration. Therefore, companies must pay attention to key indicators in analyzing their present situation to identify areas where AR could drive more value.

**Interaction** - The best AR solution must be more than just a visualization tool. Interactivity makes digital data actionable in operations, allowing the chosen solution to significantly improve operator work efficiency.

**Hardware** - The compatibility of AR solutions with available market devices can dictate which configurations would work better for certain companies. Many AR solutions only work with one type of technology; however, some of the reputed AR Solution providers, like Delmia Augmented Experience, work with many different platforms. This gives customers the flexibility to choose hardware that work best for them.

**Deployment** - Many solutions fail to go beyond the proof of concept (POC) phase, so manufacturers must ensure that their chosen AR provider has experience in industrial deployment and integration of the solution.

**Interoperability**: Manufacturers also need to ensure that their chosen AR solution can interact and integrate with their existing information systems—particularly their manufacturing execution system (MES)—to realize meaningful changes. End-to-end integration should be a key feature for AR solutions, ensuring a smooth deployment process for users no matter what systems they rely on. Connection - Companies should also consider a solution’s capacity to interact with IIoT and third-party technologies such as voice control or advanced inspection algorithms. This ensures the solution can be customized to a company’s specific needs and innovation strategy.
